---
name: SKKNI building governance
description: Product scope and role-modeling direction for NEXUS-FM.
---

NEXUS-FM is primarily a governance system for the execution, operation, inspection, and maintenance of buildings and warehouses. Its workforce model should accommodate construction job classifications, qualification levels, and competency references based on the supplied SKKNI list.

For construction-business context, support the general business nature, BUJKN/BUJKA business categories, and the BG building classification. BG001–BG009 identify building-construction subclassifications, each with small, medium, or large business qualification. Keep this business/project classification separate from personnel competencies and system access roles.

Also support the BS civil-construction catalog (BS001–BS020) as optional organization, license, contract, project, and infrastructure-asset scope. All codes may exist in the legal reference catalog, but civil-specific workflows should appear only when an organization or project uses them. Preserve qualification restrictions: BS013–BS015, BS018, and BS019 allow medium/large only; the remaining supplied BS codes allow small/medium/large.

**Why:** The product was conceived around building and warehouse governance; authorization, audit, facility registries, and analytics are supporting capabilities rather than the product's main identity.

**How to apply:** Frame features and navigation around governance activities, facilities, work assignments, competency-qualified personnel, inspections, and maintenance. Keep technical access roles separate from SKKNI occupations and qualification levels. Attach BG/BS subclassification and business qualification to the relevant organization, license, contract, or project scope. Physical assets may reference the governing project scope, while workers remain linked through assignments and competency requirements.
