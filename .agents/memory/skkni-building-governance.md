---
name: SKKNI building governance
description: Product scope and role-modeling direction for NEXUS-FM.
---

NEXUS-FM is primarily a governance system for the execution, operation, inspection, and maintenance of buildings and warehouses. Its workforce model should accommodate construction job classifications, qualification levels, and competency references based on the supplied SKKNI list.

**Why:** The product was conceived around building and warehouse governance; authorization, audit, facility registries, and analytics are supporting capabilities rather than the product's main identity.

**How to apply:** Frame features and navigation around governance activities, facilities, work assignments, competency-qualified personnel, inspections, and maintenance. Keep technical access roles separate from SKKNI occupations and qualification levels.
