name: SKKNI building governance
description: Product scope, operational emphasis, and role-modeling direction for TATABANGUN.
---

The product is named **TATABANGUN** and is now positioned as **Sistem Tata Kelola & Eksekusi Teknis Gedung, Gudang, dan Infrastruktur**, with the tagline **Eksekusi Teknis Presisi. Kepatuhan Terjamin.** Product emphasis is 70–80% daily technical field operations and 20–30% automated administrative compliance. The primary users are construction-service professionals: field executors, technicians, supervisors, site engineers, experts, and managers.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected the earlier administration-heavy direction because SKKNI job classifications describe construction professions whose daily value comes from executing and supervising technical work, not from operating administrative records.

**How to apply:** Prioritize mobile-friendly field execution, facility and asset access, technical checklists, evidence capture, inspection, maintenance, punch lists, commissioning, and handover. Keep SKKNI, SBU, and regulatory checks as background eligibility gates and passive audit evidence rather than the dominant daily navigation.

For construction-business context, support the general business nature, BUJKN/BUJKA business categories, and the BG building classification. BG001–BG009 identify building-construction subclassifications, each with small, medium, or large business qualification. Keep this business/project classification separate from personnel competencies and system access roles.

Also support the BS civil-construction catalog (BS001–BS020) as optional organization, license, contract, project, and infrastructure-asset scope. All codes may exist in the legal reference catalog, but civil-specific workflows should appear only when an organization or project uses them. Preserve qualification restrictions: BS013–BS015, BS018, and BS019 allow medium/large only; the remaining supplied BS codes allow small/medium/large.

Support specialist construction classifications as legal and work-package references: KK001–KK016 (special construction), KP001–KP002 (prefabrication), PA001 (equipment rental), PB001/PB003/PB004/PB005/PB007/PB009/PB010/PB011 (building completion), and PL001–PL008 (preparation). A dash in the supplied qualification field means unspecified in that source; do not convert it into a small/medium/large entitlement without an authoritative rule.

**Why:** The product is centered on technical execution and governance for buildings, warehouses, and infrastructure facilities; authorization, audit, construction classifications, and analytics support that work rather than replacing it.

**How to apply:** Frame features and navigation around governance activities, facilities, work assignments, competency-qualified personnel, inspections, and maintenance. Keep technical access roles separate from SKKNI occupations and qualification levels. Attach BG/BS and specialist subclassifications to the relevant organization, license, contract, project, or work package. Physical assets may reference the governing project scope, while workers remain linked through assignments and competency requirements.
