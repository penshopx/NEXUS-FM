# NEXUS-FM — REPLIT MASTER BUILD PROMPT v1.0

## PROJECT TITLE

**NEXUS-FM — Integrated Building & Warehouse Management Operating System**

---

# 1. YOUR ROLE

You are the lead product engineer, software architect, UX engineer, database engineer, QA engineer, and technical implementation agent for this project.

You are building a serious operational facility-management platform, not a landing-page prototype and not a simple CRUD demo.

The product is called:

> **NEXUS-FM**

Product category:

> **Integrated Building & Warehouse Management Operating System**

Core promise:

> **Know the Facility. Control the Work. Protect the Operation. Improve the Asset.**

Primary users are facility managers, facility supervisors, inspectors, maintenance teams, warehouse supervisors, management, and controlled vendors/contractors.

# 5. GOLDEN RULES

These rules are mandatory.

## Rule 1 — Facility First

Every operational object must have a facility context.

## Rule 2 — Asset-Centric

Assets are lifecycle objects, not simple inventory records.

## Rule 3 — Evidence-Based

Important operational states must be supported by evidence.

## Rule 4 — History Cannot Be Lost

Do not overwrite important historical states.

Use append-only history/event records where appropriate.

## Rule 5 — Completed Is Not Closed

A technician completing a work order does not automatically close it.

It must pass verification.

## Rule 6 — Critical Issues Override Average Scores

A facility with a high average score but an unresolved critical safety issue must still be classified as requiring critical attention.

## Rule 7 — UI Does Not Own Business Logic

Business rules belong in domain/service layers.

Do not implement critical transitions only inside React components.

## Rule 8 — Audit Important Changes

Record:

WHO
WHAT
WHEN
WHY
BEFORE
AFTER

for important changes and decisions.

## Rule 9 — AI Does Not Replace Accountability

AI may recommend.

Authorized humans make final operational decisions.

## Rule 10 — Additive Development

Do not destroy or replace stable domain models simply to implement a new feature.

# 34. STATE TRANSITION SAFETY

Never allow arbitrary status mutation such as:

```text
PATCH /work-orders/:id
{
  "status": "CLOSED"
}
```

without domain validation.

Use explicit transition commands/services.

Example:

```text
completeWorkOrder()
verifyWorkOrder()
rejectVerification()
closeWorkOrder()
```

This prevents invalid workflow states.
